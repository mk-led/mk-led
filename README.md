## About

MK-LED is an LED display company based in **Krishnagiri, Tamil Nadu, India**, with **20+ years of industry experience**. We take LED displays from the first site survey to long-term support: display design, structural fabrication, installation, commissioning, maintenance and repair.

## What we do

| | |
|---|---|
| **LED displays** | P2, P2.5, P3 and P4 series · Indoor · Outdoor · Event · Advertising · Custom |
| **Fabrication** | Custom frames, mounting systems and outdoor structures built for each display |
| **Installation** | Site survey, mounting, cabling, calibration and commissioning by our own crews |
| **Support** | Preventive maintenance, repair, AMC and on-call support, 24×7 |

**Where our displays are used:** corporate offices, conference and control rooms, retail and showrooms, auditoriums, places of worship, hotels, schools, advertising, building façades, stadiums and events.
